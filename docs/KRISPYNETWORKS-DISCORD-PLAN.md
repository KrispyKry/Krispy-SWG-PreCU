# KrispyNetworks Discord plan

KrispyNetworks is the shared community hub for two separate projects:

- KrispyXI: Vana'diel Expeditions / LandSandBoat
- Krispy SWG: Clone Wars / SWGEmu Core3

KingsConquer is intentionally excluded from this launch.

## Roles

Recommended role order:

1. Owner
2. Administrator
3. Moderator
4. Developer
5. QA Lead
6. KrispyXI Lead
7. SWG Lead
8. Verified Tester
9. KrispyXI Player
10. SWG Player
11. Verified Member
12. Bots

Use project roles for access and notification routing. Do not give a project role staff permissions.

## Permission model

- `@everyone`: view `#welcome-and-rules` only.
- `Verified Member`: community channels and shared announcements.
- `KrispyXI Player` / `KrispyXI Lead`: FFXI channels.
- `SWG Player` / `SWG Lead`: SWG channels.
- `Verified Tester`: shared testing channels and assigned playtest channels.
- `Moderator` and above: moderation tools and staff channels as needed.
- `Staff Operations`: hidden from all non-staff roles.

Project channels remain separate even though the community shares one Discord. FFXI and SWG support, development, patch notes, and playtest coordination must not be mixed.

## Compliance rules to pin

- No `.tre` files, client executables, proprietary client assets, leaked source, or copyrighted game data may be uploaded or linked.
- Do not request or distribute credentials, server secrets, private IPs, database dumps, or production configuration.
- SWG is an unofficial private server based on SWGEmu Core3; it is not operated or endorsed by SWGEmu.
- SWG server-side source required for the public release must remain available in the public repository under the applicable AGPL obligations.
- No selling of accounts, items, credits, or access.
- Use the bug-report channel for reproducible reports; do not post secrets or private player data.
